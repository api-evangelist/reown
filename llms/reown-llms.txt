# Reown Docs

> Reown (formerly WalletConnect) developer documentation. Build seamless onchain apps with AppKit — an all-in-one SDK for wallet connections, transactions, email & social logins, on-ramp, swaps, and notifications — and use Reown Cloud to configure projects, analytics, and APIs.
